The Chef's Journey

Discipline, Knife Artistry & Flavour Harmony

"Food that looks as exquisite as it tastes begins with strict station hygiene and respectful ingredient preparation."

I am a disciplined young chef with one year of practical kitchen experience in multi-cuisine environments and formal training from Tommy Miah’s Hospitality Management Institute (City & Guilds). My professional background spans intense service shifts, preparing ingredients for Japanese, Italian, Thai, Korean, Indian, and Bengali kitchens.

During my culinary internship at Ascott Place, Baridhara, Dhaka, I learned high-standard hospitality workflows alongside seasoned executive chefs. Whether handling sushi slicing, simmering authentic ramen broth bases, kneading fresh pizza dough, or controlling flame intensity on a commercial wok, I bring meticulous focus, punctuality, and calm under pressure.

Professional Philosophy

My Kitchen Code

Three non-negotiable principles that govern every station, knife cut, and dish that leaves my hands.

01

Hygiene First

Clean hands, spotless cutting boards, sterilized knives, and zero tolerance for cross-contamination. Temperature monitoring and safe storage preserve ingredient integrity and guest trust.

02

Precision

Uniform knife cuts for even cooking, consistent portion weights, accurate seasoning ratios, and strict adherence to recipe procedures under high-pressure kitchen service.

03

Presentation

Every plate is a visual promise of quality. Edges wiped clean, textures arranged with architectural balance, and vibrant colors that stimulate appetite before the first bite.
